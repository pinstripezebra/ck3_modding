[h2]Overview[/h2]
Elder Magic adds a progression-driven magic system to Crusader Kings III, including magic traits, education, and spellcasting system. The core goal of this mod is to enable Mage themed roleplaying within CK3.


Characters with the innate potential to wield magic, represented by 10 levels of wizard potential traits, can study different lores of magic, unlocking the ability to cast spells as well as passive bonuses. Mage themed innovations, men at arms units, and building add additional immersion.

[h2]Magic Progression[/h2]
[list]
[*] 5 Lores of Magic — Fire, Earth, Water, Death, and High Magic — each with XP-tracked traits (25 → 50 → 75 → 100) and an Ascendant tier for masters
[*] Magic Potential — 10 genetic tiers determining a character's innate magical gift, boosting lifespan, prowess, and mana
[*] Magic Education — 5 childhood tiers shaping magical aptitude
[/list]

[h2]Mana & Spellcasting[/h2]
Characters regenerate a mana pool and cast spells from an in-menu Spellbook as their Lore XP grows.

[h2]Learning & Teaching Magic[/h2]
[list]
[*] Pilgrimage of Magic — a risky decision to awaken a new Lore
[*] Study Lore of Magic — grind XP over time, at the risk of injury or a Mana-Scarred trait
[*] Teach Lore of Magic — mentor another character into your Lore via a dedicated scheme
[*] Wardship — wards and children of a lore-holding guardian naturally inherit that Lore as they grow up
[/list]

[h2]Two Magic Lifestyles[/h2]
[list]
[*] Light Magic — Battlemage and Scholar perk trees: combat prowess, mage regiment bonuses, scholarly buffs
[*] Dark Magic — Necromancy perk tree: life-draining power and undead army bonuses, at a moral cost
[/list]

[h2]Men-at-Arms & Buildings[/h2]
[list]
[*] Wandering Mages, Mage Squadron, and Mage Regiment — magic combat units
[*] Skeleton Warriors and Corpse Colossus — necromantic undead units
[*] Elemental Tower and Necromancer Sanctum — 5-level building chains unlocked by cultural traditions
[/list]

[h2]Cultural Traditions & Doctrines[/h2]
[list]
[*] Sorcerous Heritage and Necrotic Reanimators traditions unlock matching buildings, units, and bonuses
[*] New Elemental Magic and Dark Magic faith doctrines (Crime / Shunned / Accepted / Virtuous), mirroring Witchcraft
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
Most magic progression scales with a character's Learning skill.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
This mod will likely have conflicts with ones that modify the character menu; there is a compatibility patch for AGOT here: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789112475
