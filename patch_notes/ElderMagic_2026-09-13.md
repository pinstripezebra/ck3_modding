# Elder Magic — Patch Notes
**v1.0.1 — since September 6, 2026**

## New: Elder Magic Lifestyle
- Added the **Light Magic** lifestyle (elemental/high magic lore) and the **Dark Magic** lifestyle (necromancy, unlocked by the Lore of Death trait).
- Light Magic gains two perk trees:
  - **Battlemage** — personal combat power leading into defensive wards and efficient casting.
  - **Scholar** — enchanted mage-regiment equipment, teaching, and study, converging on an Arcanist capstone.
- Dark Magic gains the **Necromancy** tree — Lifestealer opening into Dark Casting and Reanimator branches, converging on a Master Necromancer capstone.
- Added 6 new lifestyle focuses (Magical Might, Longevity, Learning — one set per lifestyle).

## Combat
- Wandering Mages, Mage Squadron, Mage Regiment, Skeleton Warriors, and Corpse Colossus now have proper counters (strong vs. cavalry/skirmishers, weak vs. infantry/archers).
- Mage regiments moved to a dedicated `magi` unit archetype so their bonuses no longer spill onto vanilla heavy infantry.
- Sorcerous Heritage culture tradition updated to grant its bonuses through the new `magi` archetype.

## Art
- New icon art style for perk tree nodes across both magic trees.
