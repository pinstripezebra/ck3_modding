# Elder Magic — Patch Notes
**v1.0.2 — since September 13, 2026**

## Fixes
- Fixed wardship magic lore inheritance: wards and children of a lore-holding guardian now actually learn Lore of Fire/Earth/Water/Death/High Magic over time, and get a guaranteed catch-up grant of every missing lore their guardian holds when they turn 16.
- Added a "pity timer" to the yearly lore-teaching check so repeated bad luck can no longer stall a ward's education indefinitely.

## Battlemage Tree
- Renamed **Efficient Casting** to **Defensive Casting**. It no longer reduces spell mana cost; it now purely grants a permanently lower chance of dying in battle (flavored as a 25% reduction).
- New trait icon for the associated Battle-Wary trait.

## Scholar Tree
- **Archivist** now grants +2 Learning (up from +1).
