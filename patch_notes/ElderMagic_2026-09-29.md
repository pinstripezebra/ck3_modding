# Elder Magic — Patch Notes
**v1.4.0 — since September 21, 2026**

## Removed: Pilgrimage of Magic
- Deleted the "Pilgrimage of Magic" decision, its activity, events, and localization entirely — superseded by University of Magic Visit (below).

## New: University of Magic Visit
- Replaces Pilgrimage of Magic with a proper travel Activity: pick any settlement with a completed Elemental Tower or Necromancer Sanctum and study there for 3 months.
- Visiting an Elemental Tower works toward the elemental lores (Fire, Earth, Water, High Magic); visiting a Necromancer Sanctum works toward Lore of Death. Outcome scales with the visitor's Learning skill.
- Supports optional travel companions (up to 8 guests, solo trips still work).
- Costs gold, same as the base game's University Visit.
- Only the exact settlement with the building is a valid destination — no longer spans an entire county or duchy.
- Proper arrival/study/completion flow: a themed background and the host's own portrait are now shown for the full 3-month stay, periodic flavor vignettes appear while studying, and a distinct success/failure summary event fires once the visit actually concludes.

## New: Read Lore of Magic Tome
- New decision: reading an owned "Tome of Lore of X" artifact gives a 50% chance to learn that lore, with a 10-year cooldown on failure.
- New artifacts: Tome of Fire, Earth, Water, Death, and High Magic, discoverable via the ancient-ruins lore tome event chain.
